The Facility

Built for people who train seriously.

Free weights, turf sled track, boxing bags, and a full functional rig — every station chosen for a reason, not for show.

Every piece of equipment on our floor is commercial-grade and maintained weekly — no rusted plates, no broken cables, no excuses. We keep the floor uncrowded on purpose so you're never waiting on a rack.

Showers, lockers, and free parking are included with every membership. We're a 5-minute walk from town, open early and late for people who train before or after work.
